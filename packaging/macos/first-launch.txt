Installing Photonation
======================

Drag Photonation to the Applications folder, then open it from there.

This build runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or later.


If macOS says it can't check or verify Photonation
--------------------------------------------------

This copy isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. You only
have to approve it once.

1. Open Photonation. When macOS blocks it, click Done or OK.
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the Photonation
   message. Confirm with your password if asked.

On macOS 14 and earlier you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open.

Or paste this into Terminal, then open the app as usual:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Photonation.app


Uninstalling
------------

Move Photonation from Applications to the Trash. Your preferences and thumbnails
are in ~/Library/Application Support/Photonation; delete that folder to remove
them too. Your photos and the edits saved beside them stay where they are.
